# Calculator Vault – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 10 November 2026

Calculator Vault ("the app") is published by Gurpreet Singh Dhillon, Dunedin, New Zealand.

## Summary
The app does **not** collect, upload, sell or share any personal data. Everything you hide stays on your phone.

## What the app stores, and where
- **Photos, videos and files you choose to hide** are copied into the app's private storage on your device and encrypted (AES-256, with the key protected by the Android Keystore). They are never sent anywhere.
- **Your secret code and recovery answer** are stored only as one-way salted hashes on your device. We cannot see or recover them.

## Internet and permissions
The app does not request the Internet permission and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. It only accesses the specific files you pick using Android's own file picker.

## Backups and deleting your data
Hidden items are excluded from Google and device backups. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, permanently deletes everything in the vault. Use "Unhide" first to keep a normal copy of anything you want to keep.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
